Petr Lavrov Experiments privacy policy

Updated 5 November 2026 · Personal tools operated by Petr Lavrov

What is accessed

Essaying uses the Google Drive scope drive.file. That scope covers only files the app creates. Essaying uses it to create and update his essays as Google Docs, and to share a view link.

Other personal tools under Petr Lavrov Experiments access only Petr's own Google account. They are not used for anyone else's data.

Where it is kept

Sign-in is stored locally on his Mac. Essay files he publishes stay in his Google account. The tools do not sell or share that data.

Stop access

Access can be removed at Google Account permissions. That stops future access. Docs already created stay in the account until he deletes them.
