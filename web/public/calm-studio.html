Calm Studio

A morning show about my own projects and ideas.

Calm Studio is a personal tool operated by Petr Lavrov. It prepares finished videos on his computers and can upload selected episodes to his own YouTube channel.

How the YouTube connection works

  1. The owner connects his Google account and selects his YouTube channel.
  2. The uploader checks the channel, uploads a finished episode as private, and adds it to the private Morning Show playlist.
  3. The owner watches the episode in YouTube while signed in to that account.

The connection is configured through the owner's local application. This public page explains the tool and its data use.
